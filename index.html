Our Story

A Girl, A Dream, A Cup Of Coffee

It started in a tiny kitchen with a second-hand espresso machine and a big dream. Hannah was just nineteen when she roasted her first batch of beans — carefully, nervously, and with a heart full of hope.

She believed coffee wasn't just a drink; it was a moment of warmth, a reason to pause, a way to bring people together. So she opened a small cart on the corner of Maple Street, serving cups of joy to early commuters.

Five years later, that little cart has grown into Hannah Coffee — a beloved brand known for freshly roasted 100% Arabica beans, handcrafted drinks, and a cozy space that feels like home. But the heart of it all hasn't changed: every cup is still made with love.
